Welcome

Ban Oudomphong Farm Stay

Set among fields and water on the edge of Udon Thani, Ban Oudomphong is a place to slow down.

Our family-run farm stay brings together the warmth of traditional Isan life and the comfort today's travellers expect. Mornings begin with birdsong over the lake, and days are spent exploring the countryside by ATV, drifting across the water by boat, or casting a line from the bank. In the evening, our kitchen serves fresh, home-cooked Thai food.

Whether you are travelling from Bangkok for the weekend or from across the world, you will feel at home here.

Fly into Bangkok and connect to Udon Thani in about an hour. Experience rural Thai life with English-speaking hosts, and combine your stay with Laos, the Mekong and the hidden gems of Northeast Thailand. Contact us to arrange airport transfers.
