Our Restaurant

Home-Cooked Flavours of Isan

Our restaurant sits within the farm stay grounds, overlooking the fields and the lake.

Every dish is cooked to order in the Isan home-style tradition, using fresh herbs, local produce from the surrounding farms and markets, and fish from the region's lakes. We serve breakfast, lunch and dinner, and we are happy to adjust the spice level to suit every guest, from gentle to authentically "Thai hot".
